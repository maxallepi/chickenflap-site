Chicken Flap

A free endless arcade game for Android · com.chickenflap.game
What it is: Chicken Flap is a fast, one-tap arcade game. Tap the screen to flap, steer your chicken through gaps in the pipes, collect coins, and see how far you can get before the next crash.

What the game does

  • Endless flying gameplay — one-tap controls, procedurally generated pipe layouts, six visual worlds.
  • Four difficulty levels — Easy, Normal, Hard, and Impossible, each with its own best score.
  • Unlockables — earn coins by playing to unlock chicken skins, power-ups, and checkpoint continues. There are no in-app purchases.
  • Leaderboards and achievements — optional Google Play Games sign-in lets you compare scores with other players and unlock 12 in-game achievements.
  • Ads — the game is free and supported by banner and interstitial advertising served by Google AdMob.

Why the app asks you to sign in with Google

Chicken Flap uses Google Play Games Services for two optional features: leaderboards and achievements. If you choose to sign in, the app requests access to:

  • Your Play Games player profile — your player name and avatar, so your leaderboard entry is identifiable to other players.
  • Your Play Games player ID — used only to submit your scores and store your achievement progress against your own account.

The app does not read your email address, contacts, files, calendar, or any other Google data. Sign-in is entirely optional: if you skip it, the game remains fully playable and keeps your scores locally on your device. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
